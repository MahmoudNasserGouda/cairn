OCR sandbox spike

Settles the one unknown ADR-0028 named: can an opaque-origin sandboxed iframe load the WASM and model assets it needs from our own origin? The frame below is embedded with sandbox="allow-scripts" and no allow-same-origin, so it has no access to this page's storage.

This page deliberately sits on the application origin, under the /* policy, because that is what will embed the sandbox in production. An earlier version lived under /ocr/ and inherited that path's default-src 'none', which forbids framing anything at all — a fair result, and the wrong test.
