OCR sandbox spike

Settles the one unknown ADR-0028 named: can an opaque-origin sandboxed iframe load the WASM and model assets it needs from our own origin? The frame below is embedded with sandbox="allow-scripts" and no allow-same-origin, so it has no access to this page's storage.

This page deliberately sits on the application origin, under the /* policy, because that is what will embed the sandbox in production. An earlier version lived under /ocr/ and inherited that path's default-src 'none', which forbids framing anything at all — a fair result, and the wrong test.

What the sandbox document is served

Read back with fetch, because the bug that produced this page's first empty result was an inherited header: Cloudflare applies every matching rule rather than the most specific one, so /ocr/* was silently keeping X-Frame-Options: DENY from /* and the frame could not load at all.

What the sandbox rendered for itself

Read with your eyes from the frame's own DOM rather than through postMessage. Three outcomes, and they mean different things:

  • Box has PASS/FAIL lines — the script ran. This is the answer.
  • Box is blank and the list above says the frame loaded — the document was served but its script was refused. That is the real negative.
  • Box is blank and the list says the frame never loaded — a header stopped it. Nothing here is about WebAssembly; fix the header and re-run.
